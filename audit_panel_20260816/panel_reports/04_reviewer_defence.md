# Panel 3 — Reviewer defence (JCL / TRIPOD / PROBAST)

Source: [Panel reviewer defence](9bac3970-ac35-4a56-942e-19d30309499c)

**Verdict: REFUTE for validated / externally validated / clinically useful / superior-to-published-models claims**

## High-priority objections adopted
1. Welsh primary uses undated HTN/DM/smoke despite measured post-event BP contamination.
2. Welsh selection excludes 659 for operational follow-up, including 10 dated incident events.
3. Operational equation differs by cohort (`usable()` drops dm/smoke in Wales) — not one frozen equation.
4. DCA documentation overclaims; comparator DCA not implemented; same-sample baseline hazard.
5. Welsh optimism “family-cluster bootstrap” contradicted by individual resampling in `34`.
6. Comparator superiority not established outside selected UKB cells; Montreal is prevalent score.
7. Multiplicity: 24 cells, 6 confirmatory; Wales 5y EPV 6.3.

## Exact safe claims
- Exploratory internal model-development exercise, not clinical deployment.
- OOF discrimination 0.708 UKB / 0.757 Wales full follow-up (descriptive).
- Selected UKB published-score comparisons favourable; not instrument superiority or clinical utility.
- UKB→Wales transport C=0.725; reverse 0.660 — transport estimates, not independent external validation.

## Exact limitations that must travel with any number
- Welsh risk-factor status may post-date the event.
- Welsh inclusion depends on post-baseline operational follow-up.
- UKB event dates often not attributable to a single ASCVD component.
- Comparators evaluated outside or adapted from original settings.
- No independent external validation or valid comparative DCA.
- Wales 5y sparse/exploratory.
- Calibration/optimism not deployable absolute-risk evidence.
