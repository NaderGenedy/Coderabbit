## Model-level agreement

- **Not submission-ready:** Kimi, Grok, and Claude all judged the present manuscript unsuitable for a high-impact cardiovascular/lipid journal. All identified the unfilled ethics, legal basis, funding, conflicts, PPI, data-controller, authorship/affiliation, and code/model-availability fields as desk-rejection triggers. **Manuscript-grounded:** the placeholders and the explicit “not submission-ready” statement are present.
- **Welsh outputs are internally incoherent:** all three identified the corrected 1,169/102 frame being mixed with pre-rescue 1,159/92 discrimination/calibration outputs. They agree that all Welsh numerical artefacts must be regenerated from one pipeline before they can support a finding. **Manuscript-grounded:** Tables 3 and 5 and their footnotes display both frames.
- **Transport is not independent external validation:** all three endorsed the manuscript’s restraint on this point but judged the abstract/Key points emphasis on C=0.725/0.660 too strong, particularly without uncertainty and with Welsh predictor-timing and endpoint differences.
- **Uncertainty is incomplete:** all three require confidence intervals for transport C-statistics; the Grok debate adopts Claude’s stricter point that *every* reported C-statistic needs an interval, while recognising that the paired ΔC values already have intervals.
- **FH-Risk-Score result is a tie:** all three accept +0.015 (95% CI −0.011 to 0.040; Holm p=0.5018) as a tie, not equivalence or non-inferiority. They also agree no five-year comparison survives Holm correction.
- **The question overstates the design:** “at least as well as” implies non-inferiority without a prespecified margin. All three recommend a descriptive comparative-ranking question instead.
- **SAFEHEART/Montreal headlines need estimand qualifiers:** all agree SAFEHEART-RE was applied in an incident primary-prevention frame with previous ASCVD fixed at zero and often treatment-suppressed LDL-C; Montreal was developed for prevalent disease. The significant full-follow-up deltas therefore do not establish intrinsic superiority in intended clinical use.
- **UK Biobank competing-risk analysis is missing:** all three regard “did not execute” as an unresolved implementation failure that blocks definitive absolute-risk interpretation. Grok/Claude grade publication of baseline survival and worked examples as a safety concern; Kimi grades the same gap MAJOR rather than uniformly FATAL.
- **No clinical utility:** all agree there is no comparative decision-curve/net-benefit evidence. The de-risking prohibition should remain; “candidate first-line ranking layer” and synonyms of usefulness should be deleted.
- **Lipid nulls are not causal nulls:** all agree the non-significant lipid-derived terms do not contradict LDL causality. They differ over how interpretable the nulls are, but agree that treatment distortion, restricted/mild phenotype, and measurement error must be foregrounded.
- **Reference apparatus requires repair:** all three identify 19 uncited references, out-of-window evidential citations, and a mismatch involving ref 10. Meta-language and internal pipeline/file provenance must be removed from the manuscript.
- **Correct strengths should be preserved:** tie rule, Holm control, strict complete-input policy, comparator worked-example reproduction, refusal of “MACE”, and refusal of independent-validation/utility claims are consistently praised. Claude additionally established from code that comparator comparisons used out-of-fold CALON-C predictions.

## Specialist-seat agreement within models

- **Biostatistician seats:** converge on the Welsh frame lock, intervals on performance, the non-inferiority error, incomplete competing-risk analysis, specification-search optimism, and the distinction between transport and validation. They consistently treat paired/OOF evaluation, Holm control, and the tie rule as genuine strengths.
- **Cardiologist seats:** converge on patient-safety framing: the score must never de-risk a carrier or defer guideline-directed treatment; low internally derived absolute risks must not be presented as deployable HeFH risks; endpoint differences and post-event Welsh measurements weaken clinical interpretation.
- **Lipid-medicine seats:** converge on three points: `log(non-HDL-C × age)` is not cholesterol-years/lifetime cumulative exposure; fixed treatment back-calculation (MAE 1.20 mmol/L, r=0.32) can attenuate lipid effects; and a null Lp(a) result in this frame cannot justify omitting clinically indicated Lp(a) measurement.
- **Editor-in-Chief seats:** converge on triage realities: placeholders, mixed frames, pipeline voice, and a title/abstract that overstate the population or comparison can cause desk rejection before methodological strengths are considered. All favour a specialist/methods or implementation paper over a high-impact clinical risk-equation paper.
- **Within-model unanimity is strongest on:** non-submission, Welsh regeneration, no utility/de-risking claim, and the need to align title/abstract with the actual carrier flag. It is weakest on whether Wales remains usable as a stress test, whether the absolute-risk equation may remain after caveating, and what the recoverable paper should be.

## Recommendations requiring live-code/data verification

- **Carrier-definition rebuild:** classify LDLR variants from sequence/ClinVar/ACMG and report carrier frequency, variant classes, and LDL-C distribution versus non-carriers. The manuscript cannot establish whether the flag represents P/LP HeFH. This is the highest-priority data question.
- **Welsh risk-set rebuild:** apply an inclusion/censoring rule based only on baseline information, not event occurrence; report sensitivity to the former rescue rule and obtain the administrative end-of-recording date.
- **Intervals on performance:** bootstrap every internal, comparator, and transport C and the directional transport difference; code is needed because the manuscript provides only ΔC intervals.
- **Run UKB competing risk:** fix the `death`-column guard, report competing deaths and 5-/10-year cumulative incidence, then reconsider S0/worked examples and KM calibration.
- **Verify out-of-fold comparison identity:** Claude reports this from `code/33_CALON_C.py`; independently confirm the exact code path and frozen artefact used in the authoritative run before adding it as a strength.
- **Verify Welsh comparison identity:** Claude reports a seven-term local OOF refit, not frozen UKB CALON-C. Confirm in code/results and correct the abstract/Methods accordingly.
- **Quantify specification-search optimism:** count model generations/specifications and, if feasible, nest specification selection in resampling. The manuscript confirms iterative predecessors but not the full search process.
- **Missing-data reanalysis:** use multiple imputation with uncertainty propagation and compare with fold-wise median imputation, especially for lipid coefficients and concordance decomposition.
- **Lp(a) sensitivity:** avoid treating nmol/L÷2.15 as exact; rerun comparator thresholds across defensible assay/molar ranges or use assay-specific metadata. Determine whether differential conversion materially favours CALON-C.
- **Comparator completeness:** report all strict, ten-year, and Lp(a)-omitted cells, plus row-specific n/events and both C intervals; verify that omitted ten-year results do not reverse interpretation.
- **Endpoint sensitivities:** run harmonised hard-coronary/component analyses where supportable, with interval-first interpretation; assess the effect of ambiguous dates and endpoint mismatch.
- **Kinship/ancestry/fairness:** distinguish “not extracted” from “unavailable”; if source data permit, use family-aware uncertainty and ancestry-stratified performance rather than presenting frame choices as intrinsic UKB limitations.
- **Penalised-model inference:** verify whether Table 2 Wald-type CIs are valid under the ridge implementation; bootstrap or use a penalisation-aware method if not.

## Ranked manuscript repairs

1. **Resolve the study population/estimand.** Rebuild LDLR status from verifiable variant classification; if impossible, retitle/reframe as an unvalidated `ldlr_carrier`-flag study and remove HeFH-equivalence language. This precedes interpreting comparator performance.
2. **Rebuild Wales from one baseline-defined risk set and one pipeline.** Eliminate outcome-conditional inclusion or bound it explicitly; regenerate every Welsh display and never juxtapose KM and CIF from different frames.
3. **Remove deployable absolute-risk material pending UKB competing-risk analysis.** Fix the code, report CIF, and reassess calibration before restoring S0/worked examples to the main text.
4. **Complete governance and submission fields.** Ethics/legal basis, authorship/affiliations, funding, conflicts, PPI, data controller, code/model availability, word count, and Welsh end date are non-negotiable.
5. **Add uncertainty to every performance estimate.** Provide 95% CIs for all C-statistics and the transport-direction difference; delete/soften asymmetry if indistinguishable.
6. **Rebuild the comparative story around intended estimands.** Foreground the FH-Risk-Score tie and five-year Holm null; qualify SAFEHEART in the same sentence; demote Montreal to a cross-estimand benchmark; remove stealth non-inferiority and universal tally language.
7. **Correct model identity and validation reporting.** State OOF use if code-confirmed; state that Welsh head-to-head used a seven-term local refit if confirmed; quantify specification-search optimism and stop presenting programme iteration as prespecification.
8. **Repair lipid measurement/construct claims.** Rename the log lipid–age term, foreground r=0.32/MAE 1.20, use multiple-imputation and Lp(a)-conversion sensitivities, and do not interpret lipid/Lp(a) nulls as biological or clinical evidence.
9. **Recast the paper’s contribution.** Choose explicitly among a methods/comparator-audit paper, a setting-transfer/evaluability paper, or a new risk-equation paper; current evidence most securely supports comparator evaluability/implementation and setting dependence, not clinical superiority or utility.
10. **Convert the audit document into journal prose and repair citations.** Purge pipeline/meta-language, move traceability to supplementary/data-availability material, correct ref 9/ref 10, verify load-bearing numerical citations, replace out-of-window evidence, and cite or delete all 19 orphans.

The critical path is population validity → Welsh risk-set/source lock → competing-risk and uncertainty reruns → intended-estimand rewrite → governance/citation/prose completion. Because two debate responses and the final Cursor chair are missing, paper identity and several severity judgements remain genuine dissent rather than settled panel conclusions.
